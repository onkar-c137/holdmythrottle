01 — Inside the Kit
The Complete Kit

Three parts.
One system.

HMT connects to your bike using ports that already exist — no cutting, no permanent changes. Remove it in an hour. The whole kit ships calibrated for your exact model.

OBD-II Speed Reader

Speed from
the source.

The OBD reader plugs directly into your bike's diagnostic port and streams real-time speed data from the ECU. No wheel sensors. No GPS. Just the data your bike already generates, live.

Throttle Hold Module

Holds the
cable open.

The hold module routes inline through your throttle cable. When cruise is active it maintains exact cable tension electronically — your hand opens completely. Touch the brake and it releases in milliseconds.

Handlebar Switch

Three buttons.
Full control.

SET locks your current speed. RESUME returns you to cruise after braking. CANCEL cuts everything instantly. The switch clamps to your bar — all three within thumb reach, eyes-forward.
